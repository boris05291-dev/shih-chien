Founder

Founder: Mr. Tung-Min Shieh

Earnest practice, from self-cultivation to peace in the world

The founder of the university, Mr. Tung-Min Shieh, deeply embraced the traditional Chinese philosophy of cultivating oneself, regulating the family, governing the state and bringing peace to the world. Believing that the family is the foundation of the nation, and that a strong, prosperous country must begin with healthy families, he resolved in 1957 to establish a school of home economics. On March 26 of the following year, the first home economics school in the Republic of China, the private Shih Chien College of Home Economics, was founded. Mr. Shieh also set out the educational philosophy of "earnest practice, from self-cultivation to peace in the world," and the school was therefore named "Shih Chien," meaning practice. He explained that the name expresses the philosophy of life advocated by Wang Yangming: the unity of knowledge and action, acting as soon as one knows.

Career

  • President, Shih Chien College of Home Economics
  • Governor, Taiwan Provincial Government
  • Vice President of the Republic of China
  • Member, Central Standing Committee of the Kuomintang
  • Senior Advisor to the President
